# WikiCampus Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-11-21

WikiCampus is a student project for sharing study resources, discussions, and course groups.

## Information used by the app

When you sign in, WikiCampus uses your email address to send and verify a one-time login code. The current prototype keeps login codes and active sessions in server memory. Discussion posts and study-group information are stored by the project server in its SQLite database so they remain available after a restart.

## Chatbot and Groq

If you use the WikiCampus guide chatbot, the text of your question is sent from the WikiCampus server to the Groq API to produce an answer. The app sends the question text only; it does not send your email address, login token, or stored posts and group records as chatbot context. The app does not save chatbot conversations in its database. Groq processes the question to provide the response under the terms and privacy practices that apply to its API service.

## Choices and contact

Do not include passwords, private contact details, or other sensitive information in a post or chatbot question. For a privacy question or a request about project data, contact the WikiCampus project owner through the contact method listed on the app's store page.
